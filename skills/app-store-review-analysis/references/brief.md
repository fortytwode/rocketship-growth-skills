# Creative opportunity brief

Lead with the opportunity to test and why the evidence supports it. Adapt this outline; do not fill empty sections with guesses.

## Research coverage

Identify source files/app IDs, collection time versus review dates, store/country/rating/language coverage, filters, errors, missing fields, duplicate treatment and the actual rows inspected. Keep input-row, retained-row, analyzed-row and distinct native-identity counts separate. Without reliable native IDs, do not call row counts a count of customers.

## Customer situations

| Situation and desired change | Explicit customer evidence | Interpretation and contrary evidence | Product fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concrete trigger, job and stakes | Short exact quotes with local ledger IDs; retain store/date/rating | What is inferred, how strongly, and what challenges it | Which supplied fact supports the proposed role; missing proof |

Cover motivations and friction separately. A quote may illustrate a theme without establishing its frequency.

## Observed creative coverage

| Motivation | Inspected ad IDs and what appears | Coverage in this sample | Research limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supported customer job | Actual words/visuals reviewed | Used / underused / not observed / unknown | Missing media, date or sample scope |

Without inspected ads, omit the comparison and mark coverage unknown. Public ad longevity or prominence is not CPA/ROAS evidence.

## Ranked opportunities

For each candidate: customer situation and evidence IDs → truthful product promise → narrative premise/mechanism → hook or opening scene → product proof → test question. Explain rank using evidence, fit, novelty within observed work and feasibility; retain contradictions and proof requirements.

## Selected production briefs

- **Situation:** who is doing what, at which trigger, with which supported stakes.
- **Promise:** the narrow outcome the product facts support.
- **Opening:** a specific action, line or visual that establishes the problem.
- **Sequence:** how the situation develops and why the product enters it.
- **Proof:** the actual screen, feature action or other evidence to show; what still needs verification.
- **CTA:** the next action appropriate to the real offer.
- **Test question:** what this concept should help the team learn; no forecast is implied.

Finish with unresolved decisions that affect whether the work can be made. Do not turn operational complaints into unsupported advertising promises.
