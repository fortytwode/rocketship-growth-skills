# Creative from a reference ad

Use the user's supplied ad, context and optional data to explain what is observable and propose three useful creative directions.

## Inputs

- A video, or screenshots plus its transcript. The user may use their own video-capable assistant to inspect a video; do not require a particular product. If only a transcript is available, explicitly say visuals were not reviewed.
- Product context: audience, product, offer, constraints and why the ad was selected.
- Optional user-exported Meta CSV. No account connection or credentials are needed.

Ask only for missing information that materially affects interpretation. Without performance evidence, call it a selected reference ad, not a verified winner.

## Review

State the source ad and what was actually inspected. Describe its hook, sequence, visible treatment, offer and claims without inventing dialogue or unseen details. If the user supplied performance data, identify the metric, date window, matching row and relevant denominator or event definition. Spend can help select an ad; by itself it does not prove best ROAS or causal effectiveness. Note missing, ambiguous or incomparable data.

## Output

Return a concise source summary, metric and window when available, evidence limitations, and exactly three derivative concepts grounded in the inspected material and product context. For each concept give the changed premise, opening hook, sequence/visual direction, and why it is a reasonable variation. Do not invent efficacy or performance claims. Keep unverified claims clearly labeled for confirmation.

Ask for feedback before asset production. Generation, account access and publishing require separate requests and are outside this skill.
